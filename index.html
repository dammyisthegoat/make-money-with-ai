Beginner Roadmap

How AI Training and Evaluation Work Can Flow

Learn the general journey involved in finding, qualifying for, and completing human-in-the-loop AI work.

01 - Find the Right Type of AI Work

AI-related work can involve many different tasks. Depending on the opportunity, you might evaluate written responses, categorize information, review images or audio, or contribute specialist knowledge.

General AI response evaluation
Domain-specific annotation
Image, text, and audio review

Focus Areas

Look for opportunities that match your existing knowledge, writing ability, language skills, or professional background.

02 - Understand Guidelines & Rubrics

Many AI evaluation tasks rely on detailed instructions. Understanding the criteria and applying them consistently is an important part of doing this type of work well.

Read task guidelines carefully
Understand evaluation criteria
Practice consistent reasoning

The Important Part

Don't rush through instructions. In evaluation work, carefully understanding the rules can matter more than simply completing tasks quickly.

03 - Complete Assessments & Onboarding

Some legitimate platforms use assessments or qualification steps before granting access to projects. The exact process varies between companies and roles.

Follow assessment instructions
Demonstrate writing or reasoning skills
Complete required verification steps

Quality Matters

AI platforms may review work quality over time. Accuracy, consistency, and following project guidelines can all affect continued access to tasks.

04 - Start Completing Tasks

Once accepted into a legitimate project, workers may complete available tasks according to the platform's requirements, schedule, and payment structure.

Work through available tasks
Follow project requirements
Track your work and platform payments

Keep Expectations Realistic

Task availability, eligibility, rates, and workload can vary. Treat AI work as an opportunity to research rather than a guaranteed source of income.
