AI Job Scam Red Flag Checklist

Learn how to spot and avoid fake AI job offers, recruitment scams, upfront payment requests, and suspicious messaging apps.

Common Red Flags in Fake AI Job Offers

If you encounter any of the following scenarios during an online job search, immediately discontinue communication:

  • Requests for Upfront Payment: Legitimate companies will never ask you to pay a registration fee, buy equipment kits, or purchase software licenses to start working.
  • Guaranteed Massive Daily Income: Promises that you will make $500/day clicking buttons with zero experience are textbook marketing traps.
  • Communication on Encrypted Apps Only: If a recruiter insists on interviewing or onboarding exclusively through Telegram, WhatsApp, or Signal without a corporate email domain, be extremely suspicious.
  • Check-Cashing or Equipment Scams: Sending you a check to deposit and asking you to wire a portion back for "setup supplies" is a classic financial fraud.

Safe Practices for Job Seekers

Always verify company websites via official registry channels, check corporate LinkedIn pages, and read independent worker testimonials before submitting sensitive personal identification data.
